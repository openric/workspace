# Transfers to the National Archives

A South African government body must transfer records of enduring value to the National Archives and Records Service of South Africa (NARSSA) when their time comes, under the National Archives and Records Service of South Africa Act 43 of 1996. With the NARSSA jurisdiction pack switched on in **Settings**, the Workspace prepares those transfers.

The screens are under **Admin**, **Transfers to the National Archives** (`/jurisdiction/narssa`).

## Making a transfer

**New transfer**: give its reference and title, and the records, by their persistent IRIs, one a line. Or **package every approved transfer disposal**: every disposal approved under the records management rules with the action *transfer* becomes part of a transfer.

## The package

**Package** builds the transfer as NARSSA expects it: the records' descriptions, their files with their checksums, and a list of what is in it. Download it to send.

## Where it stands

Mark the transfer as **transmitted** when it is sent. When NARSSA answers, mark it **accepted**, with the reference of its receipt, or **returned**, with the reason.

## Export permits

**Export permits** (`/jurisdiction/export-permits`) records applications to export heritage objects under the National Heritage Resources Act, as the South African Heritage Resources Agency asks: the records concerned, the purpose, the documents that go with the application, and the permit when it is issued.

## Zimbabwe

An institution in Zimbabwe switches on the NAZ, NMMZ and CDPA packs instead, for the National Archives of Zimbabwe, National Museums and Monuments of Zimbabwe and the Cyber and Data Protection Act.
