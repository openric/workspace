# Security clearance

Some institutions hold material that only cleared staff may see: government records with a security classification, police files, personnel records, matters before a court. Security clearance classifies records by level and clears accounts to a level, so a classified record reaches only those cleared to read it.

The screens are under **Research**, **Security clearance** (`/clearance`), for those who administer the institution.

## Levels

The institution sets its own classification levels, each with:

- a code, a name and a colour to show it by;
- what reading it requires: a justification for classifying, approval for access, two-factor sign-in, a longest session in hours;
- what may be done with it: whether downloads, printing and copying are allowed.

Levels are ordered: an account cleared to a level may read that level and every level below it.

## Compartments

A compartment is a need-to-know group on top of the levels, such as a particular investigation or a board's papers. It has its own code, the lowest level it applies from, and whether it needs a briefing. An account must be cleared into the compartment, as well as to the level, to read what is in it.

## Clearing an account

Choose an account, its level and its compartments, and grant it, with an end date if it should lapse. A clearance can be changed or revoked at any time. Every grant, change and revocation is kept in its history and in the audit log.

## Classifying a record

On a unit of description, choose its level and its compartments. Where the level asks for one, give the justification. **Declassify** takes it off again, with a note.

## What classification does

A classified record, its files and its hits in a search reach only an account cleared to its level and into every compartment it is in. The public never sees a classified record, in the catalogue or in any open interface, whatever its publication state.

## Requests for access

Someone not cleared can **ask** for access to one classified record, naming it by its persistent IRI. Requests wait under **Requests for access**, with their priority, until someone who administers the institution grants access for a number of hours, or denies it. Your own clearance, and your requests, are shown on the same screen.
