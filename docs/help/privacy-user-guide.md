# Privacy and POPIA

Archives hold personal information by the shelf: names, identity numbers, medical details, addresses. The Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA) governs how a South African institution handles it, and similar laws apply elsewhere. The privacy screens keep the institution's registers and help keep personal information out of what the public sees.

The screens are under **Admin**, **Privacy** (`/privacy`). They need the right to manage privacy.

## Registers

| Register | What it keeps |
|---|---|
| Information Officers | The Information Officer and deputies, and their registration |
| Processing register | Each processing activity: its purpose, the categories of personal information and of data subject, its lawful basis and retention, submitted and approved |
| Impact assessments | Assessments of the risk of a processing activity |
| Consent | Who consented to what, when, and its withdrawal |
| Data subject requests | Requests to access, correct, delete, object to, restrict or move personal information, each with its due date |
| PAIA requests | Requests under the Promotion of Access to Information Act, with the 30 days the Act allows |
| Complaints | Complaints made, and how they were resolved |
| Security compromises | Breaches, with their notification to the Regulator and to the people affected |

Each entry has its status, and the due dates of requests are worked out from when they were received and the jurisdiction's time limit. **POPIA report** brings the registers together for the Information Officer.

## Jurisdictions

**Jurisdictions and compliance controls** (`/privacy/controls`) chooses the laws the institution works under, and their time limits. A Zimbabwean institution adds the Cyber and Data Protection Act, which brings a register of data controller licences.

## Personal information in the catalogue

On a unit of description, **Scan for personal information** looks through its description for what looks like personal information, such as identity numbers, telephone numbers, e-mail addresses and the special personal information of POPIA section 26, by the institution's patterns, and lists each with the field and its confidence.

For each finding, decide:

- **Redact a field**: the public, and anyone who may not change the record, read the redacted value; the record keeps the original. A partial redaction keeps the part you choose.
- leave it, where it is not personal information or may be published.

## Screening files

A unit's files can be marked as needing screening before the public receives them. Until someone decides, the files are held back. **Release them** once what was found is redacted, or once the risk is accepted, with a note; or withhold them. **Screening** (`/privacy/screening`) lists the files waiting.
