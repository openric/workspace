# The public catalogue

Every institution has a public catalogue at its own address. It shows what the institution has published, and nothing else: no draft, no value kept back, no file the rights or an embargo hold back.

## Its home

The home page of the catalogue opens with the institution's own introduction and how much it has published, then a search, its collections with pictures, every top-level collection and every repository. An institution can replace this with a landing page of its own design; the catalogue's own home is then at `?catalogue=1`. See [Landing pages](/help/article/landing-page-user-guide).

## A record's page

Each published unit of description has a page with:

- its description, area by area, in the visitor's language where there is one;
- where it sits in its hierarchy, with the units above and inside it;
- its creators and other access points, each linking to its own page;
- its digital copies, in the viewer that suits each;
- **Cite as**, the record ready to cite in the common styles;
- **Ask for access** where some of its files are kept back;
- links to the same record as linked data and in IIIF.

Authority records, repositories, places, activities and rules have pages of their own, each listing what it is related to.

## The graph

Every page links to the entity's **graph**: the entity and what it is related to, one step out or two. See [Records in Contexts in the Workspace](/help/article/ric-user-guide).

## Withdrawn records

A record that is withdrawn is not simply gone. Its address answers that it has been withdrawn, with when and why, and where it was merged into another or replaced, a link to that one. Anyone who cited it can still find out what happened to it.

## For the visitor

- The **clipboard** gathers records while browsing, to save as a set or export.
- The **cart** holds reproductions asked for, where the institution sells them.
- **Help**, at the foot of every page, opens the Help Center.
- The language menu switches between the languages the institution offers.

## Open data

The foot of every page links to the open interfaces: the OpenRiC API and OAI-PMH. See [Open data and harvesting](/help/article/oai-user-guide).
