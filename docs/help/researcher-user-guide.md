# Registering as a researcher

Anyone may read the public catalogue. To book a visit to the reading room, ask for reproductions, keep research collections or work on a research project, you register as a researcher.

## Registering

1. Sign up for an account, or sign in to the one you have.
2. Open **Research** (`/research`) and register: your name, how to reach you, your affiliation (such as a university, a government body or none), and what you are researching.
3. Submit. Your registration is pending until the reading room staff approve it; you are told by e-mail.

A registration can be approved, rejected, suspended or expire after a time, as the institution sets. **Research** shows yours and when it ends.

## What a researcher can do

- **Book a visit** to the reading room, with the records you want to see; see [Booking the reading room](/help/article/reading-room-booking).
- **Ask for reproductions**: copies, scans, photographs, transcriptions or certified copies; see [Reproductions](/help/article/reproduction-requests).
- **Ask for access** to files that are kept back; see [Access requests](/help/article/access-requests-user-guide).
- **Keep collections** of records, notes and bibliographies, and **save searches**.
- **Work on projects**, alone or with others, with their sources, claims and outputs; see [Research projects](/help/article/research-user-guide).
- **Cite** what you use; see [Citing a record](/help/article/cite-this-record-user-guide).

## For the reading room staff

**Researchers** (`/reading-room/researchers`) lists every registration, pending first. Open one to see what they told you, and approve, reject or suspend it, verifying their identity where the institution asks. **Kinds of researcher** (`/reading-room/types`) defines the kinds the institution distinguishes, such as academic, genealogist, student or journalist, each with how long its registration lasts and what it may book.
