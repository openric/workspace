# Loans between institutions

Members of a union lend each other items: for an exhibition, for research, for conservation. **Loans** (under **Federation**, `/federation/loans`) keeps each request and where it stands.

## Asking for a loan

**Request a loan**:

1. Choose the member that holds the item and the member that asks to borrow it. The institution itself is one of the members, so a loan can go either way.
2. Name the item: the unit of description, where the institution holds it, or the holder's reference and title for it.
3. Give the purpose and the dates it is needed from and to.
4. Save.

## Where it stands

A loan moves only along these steps:

- **requested**, then approved, declined or cancelled;
- **approved**, then in transit or cancelled;
- **in transit**, then returned.

Each move is recorded with who made it, when, and a note, such as *Insured by the borrower*. A move out of turn is refused.

## The figures

The page shows the loans by status, how many the institution asked of others and others of it, the share approved of those decided, the average days to a decision, and the members and items most asked for.

Only who administers the institution handles its loans.
