# Articles

Articles are the institution's news and stories: a new accession, an exhibition, a guide to a collection, a conservation project. They are listed at `/articles` on the public site and written under **Public site**, **Articles** (`/site/articles`), by anyone who may publish descriptions.

## Writing one

1. Choose **New article**.
2. Give its **title**; its **address** is made from the title unless you give one.
3. Put it in a **group**, such as *News* or *Conservation*, so readers can filter by it; groups already used are offered as you type.
4. Give its **author** and a short **summary** for the list.
5. Write its **text** in Markdown.
6. Choose a **cover picture**.
7. Leave it as a **draft** while you work, and save.

## Publishing

Set its status to **Published** and save. Its publication date is set the first time it is published, unless you give one; a date still to come keeps it off the public site until then, so you can prepare an article for the day of an opening.

## Pictures in the text

Under **Pictures for the text**, upload a picture to get its address, then put it in the text as `![a description](the address)`. The description is what a screen reader reads, so say what the picture shows. A picture of an article is shown only while the article is published.

## Files

**Attach a file** adds a guide, template, checklist, worksheet, report, dataset, presentation, policy, conference paper or video to the article, with its title and description. Files of the same **section** are listed together under its name, and the arrows put them in order. A video plays in the page. The heading over the files is *Files* unless you set **Heading over the files**.

Each file is kept in the object store and served only while the article is published.

## Related articles

**Link to** another article, with why they are related, to show each on the other's page. The arrows put the links in order.

## The public list

Readers see nine articles to a page, newest first, by group, or sorted oldest first, by title or by how often they were read, as tiles or a list. A read is counted once a visit, once the article has been open a few seconds, and never for staff.

## Deleting

**Delete article** removes it with its files, its comments and its links.
