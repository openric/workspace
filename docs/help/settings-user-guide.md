# Settings

**Settings** (under **Admin**, `/settings`) holds what an institution says about itself and how its Workspace behaves. It needs the right to administer the institution.

## The institution

- **Name of the institution**, and the **text in the header** of every page.
- **Logo**: PNG, JPEG or WebP, at most 1 MB, shown in the header of the public catalogue. **Remove the logo** takes it away.

## The public catalogue

- Its **heading**: by default *The catalogue of* and the institution's name.
- Its **introduction**, shown under the heading on the catalogue's home: an empty line starts a new paragraph, and a web address becomes a link.
- Whether to **show the counts** of what is published under the introduction.
- **Credits for data reused from others**, shown at the foot of the home page.

For a home page of your own design, make a landing page the default; see [Landing pages](/help/article/landing-page-user-guide).

## Languages

**Interface languages** are the languages people may choose for the screens of the Workspace and the public catalogue. English is always offered; a string with no translation in a language is shown in English.

## Images

**Watermark on the reference copies of images**: a text or image watermark, its position and opacity, put on the reference copies the public sees. The master is never watermarked, nor is a thumbnail. Changing it marks the reference copies to be made again.

## Users

Whether visitors may **ask for an account**, each request approved or rejected by an administrator under **Users**.

## Jurisdiction packs

**Jurisdiction packs** are the work a national archive or heritage law asks of the institution: South Africa's National Archives (NARSSA) and the South African Heritage Resources Agency, Zimbabwe's National Archives (NAZ), National Museums and Monuments (NMMZ) and the Cyber and Data Protection Act. A pack switched on adds its screens to the menus; switched off, it leaves them, and what it holds is kept.

## Other settings

Settings with screens of their own: **Sign-in settings** (single sign-on and two-factor sign-in), **Accession settings**, **Email**, **Webhooks**, **Federation**, **Archivematica** and the **Preservation** rules.
