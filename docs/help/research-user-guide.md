# Research projects

The Workspace is a place to work as well as a catalogue. A registered researcher has a set of tools under **Research** for gathering sources, thinking about them and writing up, alone or with others, with every source tied to the record it came from.

## Gathering

- **Collections** (`/research/collections`): named sets of records, each item with your note, to share by a link if you choose.
- **Bibliographies** (`/research/bibliographies`): reference lists of records and other works, exported as BibTeX, RIS or CSL-JSON.
- **Annotations** (`/research/annotations`): notes on a record, or on a region of an image in the viewer.
- **Notebooks** and a **diary**: your own notes, kept with the records they are about.
- **Saved searches**: see [Saved searches](/help/article/saved-searches).

## Projects

A **project** (`/research/projects`) brings it together: its question, its team and their roles, its sources, and what it produces. Within a project:

- **sources** are records and works, each assessed for reliability and triaged by relevance;
- **claims** record what the sources show, each with its evidence, and contradictions between them are found and kept in view;
- **analysis** draws timelines, maps and the network of people and places across the project's sources, and finds the same person or place named differently;
- **writing** studios help draft the project's outputs, with their citations kept to the records;
- funding, plans, ethics, milestones, impact and outputs are recorded as a project needs them.

A project can be shared with others, each with a role, or opened by a link for someone without an account.

## Working together

**Workspaces** (`/research/workspaces`) are shared spaces for a team: files, links and discussion around a body of work, with members and their roles.

## Publishing what you find

- **Submissions** (`/research/submissions`) send corrections or new information about a record to the institution, which reviews them before anything changes.
- **Journals** (`/research/journals`) and **reports** (`/research/reports`) publish what a project produced.

## Keeping it honest

Every claim, note and source in a project is tied to the record it rests on by the record's persistent IRI, so what a project says can always be checked against the archive.
