# Reports

## Standard reports

**Reports** (`/reports`) holds the reports every institution needs:

- **Descriptions**: the archival descriptions, by level, repository and publication state;
- **Authorities**: the authority records, by kind;
- **Repositories**: each repository and what it holds;
- **Taxonomies**: the terms in use, and how often;
- **Recent updates**: what was made and changed, by whom, in a period;
- **User activity**: what each person did;
- **Quality**: the gaps, such as published units without dates, a creator or a scope and content;
- **Usage**: what the public looked at and downloaded.

Each filters by repository and dates, and downloads as CSV.

## The report builder

**Report builder** (`/reports/builder`) makes reports of your own. A report has a source, such as archival descriptions, authority records, accessions or files, and sections:

| Section | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Summary | A short account in figures |
| Table | The records, with the columns you choose |
| Counts | How many, grouped by a field |
| Text | Words of your own, such as an introduction |

Filter it, sort it and preview it as you build it.

## Sharing and keeping reports

- **Export** a report as CSV, Excel, PDF or Word.
- **Schedule** it to run daily, weekly, monthly or quarterly and send itself to the people you name.
- **Archive** a run, so the report as it stood on a day is kept.
- **Share** it by a link, for someone without an account to read it.
