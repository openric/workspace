# Vendors

Material regularly leaves the strongroom for work done elsewhere: conservation, digitisation, framing, binding, pest treatment, valuation. The vendor screens keep track of who does that work and of every item that goes out to them and comes back.

The screens are under **Manage**, **Vendors** (`/vendors`).

## Vendors

A vendor is a company, an individual, an institution or a government body, active, inactive, suspended or pending approval. For each, record:

- its contact details and contacts;
- the services it offers, such as conservation, restoration, framing, digitisation, photography, binding, cleaning, pest treatment, storage materials, transport, valuation, insurance, mounting, deacidification and encapsulation;
- its documents, such as a contract, an insurance certificate or a quote.

**Services** (`/vendors/services`) lists the vendors by the service they offer.

## Transactions

A transaction is one job: the items sent, to which vendor, for which service. It moves through its statuses:

1. pending approval, then approved;
2. dispatched, received by the vendor, in progress;
3. completed, ready for collection, returned;

or cancelled along the way.

For each, record the units of description it covers, with the condition of each and the work to be done; the dates, including when it is due back; the estimated and actual cost, the invoice and its payment status; the insurance arranged for it and how it is shipped, by which courier; and the documents that go with it, such as a condition report, a quote, an invoice or a delivery note. An item that needs a valuation or insurance before it goes is flagged.

**Transactions** (`/vendors/transactions`) lists them all, filtered by status or vendor, so you can see what is out and what is overdue. **Request** starts a transaction from the vendor's page.

## Why it matters

The record of what left the building, where it went and in what state it came back is part of the custody history of each item. Each transaction keeps its own history of who moved it on, and when. To record it in an item's custody history as well, add a step to its provenance; see [Provenance and custody history](/help/article/provenance-user-guide).
