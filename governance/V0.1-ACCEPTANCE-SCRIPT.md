# V0.1 acceptance script

Draft for freezing, 2 November 2026 (roadmap phase 23b). The script an independent installer and a working archivist follow to accept V0.1, and what is kept as evidence at each step. It joins the ten steps of the security and functional audit of 1 November 2026 to the scenarios of the RiC standards gap assessment and the design review of 1 and 2 November 2026, and stands beside `V0.1-DEMONSTRATION-SCENARIO.md`, which holds the sample data.

Nobody signs a step off without its evidence. Each step names the automated test that already checks the same thing, so a failure in the walkthrough can be told from a failure in the code.

## Who

- **Installer**: someone who has not worked on the Workspace, on a clean host.
- **Archivist**: a working archivist who has not used the Workspace before.
- **Witness**: keeps the evidence, notes every defect with the steps to repeat it, and signs the dated report with the other two.

## Steps

| # | Step | Pass when | Evidence kept | Test that checks it already |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Install on a clean host, with no Heratio or AtoM running, from the README and `deploy/README.md` | The site answers, `php artisan workspace:preflight` passes, and the installer did not need the developers | The installer's notes, the preflight output, the commit installed | `InstallTest`, `PreflightTest` |
| 2 | Create a repository, a fonds, a series and an item; move the item to another series | Each keeps its IRI after the move and after a rename | The IRIs before and after | `RecordTest`, `GovernanceTest` |
| 3 | Describe a creator that changes: a body succeeded by another, a post held by two people in turn, the activity it carried out, and the mandate behind it; relate each with its dates and source | Every relation reads with its period and source, from both ends | Screens of the agent, activity and rule pages | `RicScenariosTest` S1, `RuleTest` |
| 4 | Record a disputed creator: two sources disagree; a second archivist rejects one claim with a reason; a machine-proposed creator is rejected in review | Both claims, both sources, the reason and the review stay visible to staff; the public sees the dispute as disputed and nothing of the rejected guess | The relation's assertions page; the public JSON-LD | `RicScenariosTest` S3 |
| 5 | Describe in two languages, Afrikaans after English | The English stays as written; each value carries its language in every export | The record page in each language; the RiC-O export | `InterfaceLanguageTest`, `PortabilityTest` |
| 6 | Add a paper letter, a master scan, a viewing copy and a lost carbon copy; relate them by derivation; put the master under embargo | Each carrier has its own IRI; the master's checksum shows; the lost copy stays described; the embargoed master's address is refused to the public | The instantiation pages; the refused request | `RicScenariosTest` S2, `EmbargoTest`, `PublicAccessMatrixTest` |
| 7 | Submit a draft for review; a second account approves and publishes; then try to reach the draft as a visitor, by search, the API, OAI-PMH, IIIF and an export | Only the published is reached | The refused requests and the empty searches | `PublicAccessMatrixTest`, `AccessMatrixTest` |
| 8 | Find the item by keyboard alone: in search, in the tree, and in the relationship graph's list | Reached without a mouse; the graph's list gives every relation the picture shows | A note of the keys used | `DiscoverTest` |
| 9 | Export the fonds in each format; read what each leaves out; import the RiC-O export into a second institution | What each format leaves out is listed on the export page and as JSON; the import keeps identifiers, languages, dated relations and evidence, and lists what it could not link | The left-out report; the import report | `ExportTest`, `RicScenariosTest` S5 |
| 10 | Restore the database and the files on a second host from the backup; check the files' checksums, the relations and the audit chain | Every file matches its SHA-256; `workspace:audit:verify` passes; the relations are there | The restore log and the outputs | Not yet: roadmap phase 3, the last condition of V0.1 sign-off |
| 11 | The archivist signs a dated report of the tasks done and the defects met; the witness lists each defect with the steps to repeat it | Signed | The report | - |

## What this does not test

Accessibility with a screen reader is a separate review (WS-018). Load, penetration testing and an external security review are part of gate G4, not of this script. One record described in two record sets at once (the design review's scenario E) is not in V0.1: a description has one parent and associated records beside it, and several parents are a decision still to take.
