# V0.1 demonstration scenario (draft for freezing)

Status: draft, 19 October 2026, for Dr Johan Pieterse and the archival domain lead (to be named at G0) to freeze. Once frozen it changes only through a decision record, because the G2 gate, the usability test (WS-012) and the V0.1 estimate are all measured against it.

The scenario follows the V0.1 success sentence in `SPECIFICATION.md` step by step, and adds the one relationship the roadmap asks for whose evidence changes. An archivist performs every step in the interface, with no developer, API tool or database access.

## Sample dataset

Synthetic, and labelled so on every screen and in every export: the "Riverside Parish Council" papers, about 40 records. Every name is invented; nothing is attributed to a real institution or person, a lesson from the OpenRiC demonstration record that was once presented under a real museum's name. The dataset is published with the scenario under CC BY 4.0, so a reviewer can rerun it on any instance.

| Part | Content |
|---|---|
| Hierarchy | Fonds, two series (minutes; correspondence), files, items, and one record part (a page of a minute book) |
| Agents | The council (corporate body), a clerk (person), a family that deposited papers, and two people named in a marriage entry |
| Activities | Creation of the minute books; a 1952 marriage recorded in the correspondence; the 2026 deposit |
| Places | The parish and a neighbouring town |
| Languages | Titles in English with an Afrikaans translation of the fonds title; one item in Afrikaans only |
| Files | One TIFF scan and one PDF, both synthetic, with known SHA-256 values |
| Rules | One record closed until 2030 under an access restriction; one published |

## Steps

| # | The archivist | Requirement | Passes when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Installs the Workspace on a clean server and creates the institution, the repository and their own account | WS-001 | No Heratio, Qubit or AtoM component is present |
| 2 | Builds the hierarchy, then moves one file to the other series | WS-002 | Identifiers do not change on the move; a move that would create a cycle is refused |
| 3 | Describes the fonds and one item: title, dates (creation, with type from the date-type vocabulary), scope and content, identifier, source | WS-004 | The values are saved, edited and visible in the audit trail |
| 4 | Adds the Afrikaans title of the fonds as a translation | WS-005 | The English original is kept and marked original; the translation has its own tag and state |
| 5 | Links the clerk to the creation activity, and the activity to the minute books | WS-003 | The link can be followed both ways, with who made it and when |
| 6 | Records the marriage: the activity, the two people, and a witness, each with a role from the role-type vocabulary (spouse, witness), citing the letter as evidence | WS-003, WS-006 | Each relation shows its role, its source and its review status |
| 7 | Corrects the witness: a second letter shows the witness was a different person, the parish clerk. The archivist records the corrected assertion, linking the clerk as witness, with the new letter as evidence | WS-006 | Both assertions are visible; the first is marked superseded, not deleted; the reason and the new source are recorded |
| 8 | Uploads the scan and the PDF to the item's instantiation | WS-007 | The SHA-256 is checked; the file is reachable only through the Workspace |
| 9 | Sends the item for review, approves it as a second user, and publishes it; leaves the closed record unpublished | WS-008 | The closed record and every draft language value are absent from discovery, the API, exports and the graph |
| 10 | Finds the item in public discovery, opens its graph, and follows it to the clerk and the marriage | WS-009 | The graph shows only RiC-O and OpenRiC classes, and every node opens its own description |
| 11 | Exports the fonds through a pinned OpenRiC profile | WS-010 | The export passes the probe and the term check in `conformance/README.md`; identifiers and language tags survive |
| 12 | Takes a backup, restores it on a second server, and checks the marriage relations, both witness assertions and both files | WS-011 | Every demonstration entity, assertion and file is present after the restore |

: The twelve steps of the V0.1 demonstration

Step 7 is the relationship whose evidence changes. It exercises the role (service issue #11) and competing, superseded assertions (service issue #17) together.

## Negative checks run alongside

- A user without the publish permission cannot publish (step 9).
- The closed record cannot be reached through any public channel, by search, direct IRI, file URL, export or SPARQL (step 9).
- An attempt to add a status, role or level outside its vocabulary is refused; a new value is added to the vocabulary instead (the no-enumeration constraint).

## Not in the scenario

Migration from Heratio is measured separately (WS-013, gate G3), on a difficult Heratio sample rather than this synthetic set.
